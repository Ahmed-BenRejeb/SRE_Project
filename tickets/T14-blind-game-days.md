# T14 — Blind game days
Status: todo · Arc 3

## Setup
Once a week for 4 weeks, Claude (game master mode in CLAUDE.md) or a friend injects one fault
you don't know about. You find out only through a page.

## Done when
- 4 postmortems, each with time-to-detect and time-to-mitigate.
- At least one where **no alert fired**, followed by a fix to your alerting.
- Your runbooks were updated after each game day.
