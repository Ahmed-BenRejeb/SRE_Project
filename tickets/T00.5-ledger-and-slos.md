# T00.5 — The ledger and SLOs
Status: todo · Arc 0

## Problem
Right now "no data loss" is a feeling. Every later ticket needs a way to *prove* it.

## Done when
- A command answers, for any time window: events produced by the sim, accepted by ingest,
  processed by trigger, selected, written to storage, sent to the DLQ (later), **lost**, and
  **duplicated**.
- **Test the ledger itself:** add a fault flag that drops exactly 1% of events at a hop you
  choose, or duplicates 0.5%. The ledger must report exactly that.
- The live histogram after a full replay matches your offline plot from Phase 0.3 **bin for
  bin**. This is your end-to-end correctness check.
- `design/slos.md` defines 3–4 SLOs, each with an SLI, a target, a window, and *why that number*.
  Suggestions: ingest availability, ingest p99 latency, freshness (event → visible in histogram),
  and completeness (lost = 0). You'll alert on them later, in T13.

## Constraints
The ledger must not be the bottleneck. If it adds more than 10% latency to ingest, redesign it.

## Read after
Google SRE Book, ch. 4 "Service Level Objectives". Think about how a bank reconciles two ledgers.
